WHY JIOTV MAX

Live television unlocked for big screens.

JioTV has no official desktop client, and its web portal blocks live TV streaming. JioTv Max bridges the gap.

01 THE GAP

No Official Windows Support

JioTV is locked to mobile devices. Its web portal does not stream live broadcasts, leaving PC, laptop, and monitor setups unable to watch live TV natively.

02 ZERO EXTRA COST

Works With Standard Mobile Plans

You do not need an expensive Smart TV subscription or JioFiber Set-Top Box. Any regular Jio mobile recharge bundled with basic JioTV access works out of the box.

03 ONE-STEP SETUP

Simple OTP Login

Sign in with your Jio mobile number via OTP. Whatever live channels your current plan supports on mobile are immediately available on your PC.

FEATURES

Engineered for a clean desktop experience.

Fast, responsive, and resource-friendly without the bloat of Android emulators.

1,000+ Live Channels

Instant access to national and regional sports, entertainment, news, movies, and music channels with low-latency zapping.

Full HD 1080p Playback

Crisp 1080p and 720p streams with hardware GPU decoding. Clear broadcast quality on monitors and high-resolution displays.

Live DVR Timeline

Pause live television and rewind within the stream's rolling timeshift buffer. Jump back to the live edge with one click.

No Cheap Emulator Streams

Emulators on Windows get restricted to a cheap, unwatchable quality stream. JioTv Max streams natively with GPU hardware decoding in full crisp quality.
